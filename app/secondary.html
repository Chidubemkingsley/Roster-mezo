A fill is a direct MUSD payment to the seller. The book fills all remaining lots in this interface; partial fills are available in the contract. The book freezes at maturity.

Settlement rules

What a fill actually does.

There is no clearinghouse. The buyer's MUSD moves to the seller, the seller's escrowed lots move to the buyer, and the series ledger is updated in the same transaction.

BEFORE FILL

Seller approves the book.

The order book calls transferFrom on the series to escrow exactly the listed number of lots. The seller keeps the ask price fixed until cancel or fill.

AT FILL

Buyer approves MUSD.

The buyer approves the total MUSD amount, then the book pays the seller and transfers the lots. No partial fill is offered by this interface.

AT MATURITY

Everything freezes.

Position transfers, new listings, and fills stop at expiry. Cancel any ask you do not want stuck in the book before then.

No liquidity guarantee.

An ask is not a redemption right. If no buyer fills it, the seller can cancel and exercise (if in the money) or let the position expire worthless. The live book currently has one demo listing; that is not evidence of organic demand.

01
An ask can go unfilled.

There is no bid side, market maker, or protocol buyback. The best exit may be no exit.

02
Escrowed lots are locked.

Listed positions cannot be exercised until the listing is cancelled. Cancel before maturity.

03
Price is the seller's, not a mark.

The displayed ask is not an oracle price or a guarantee of fair value. Do your own pricing.
