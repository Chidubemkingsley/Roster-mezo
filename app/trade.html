01
Exercise is manual.

If you do nothing before expiry, the position expires worthless. No keeper will exercise for you.

02
ERC-20 BTC is required.

The exercise asset is separate from native BTC trove collateral. Roster does not convert between them.

03
Premium is non-refundable.

A position can expire out of the money. Buying a put is not a guaranteed hedge or a principal-protected product.
