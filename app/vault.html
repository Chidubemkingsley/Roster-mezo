Accounting rules

Where every MUSD goes.

The vault never labels borrowed MUSD as profit. Series collateral is reserved against strike payouts; premium is earned by the writer; trove debt remains an obligation until repaid.

01 · DEPOSIT

Borrowed MUSD becomes reserve.

openAndFund borrows MUSD from Mezo, keeps a gas compensation buffer, and deposits the series portion. The series records the deposit as collateral.

02 · PURCHASE

Premium is separate from reserve.

A buyer's premium lands in the series premium balance. The series reserves the full strike for each lot; premium does not increase the payout.

03 · EXERCISE

ERC-20 BTC moves to the writer.

The holder delivers ERC-20 BTC; the series pays MUSD from reserved collateral. The writer can later withdraw the ERC-20 BTC from the vault.

04 · EXPIRY

Unsold reserve returns.

After maturity, expire() releases remaining series MUSD to the writer. It does not repay the Mezo trove or create free collateral.

Owner controls are intentionally narrow.

The owner can withdraw premium, unreserved idle MUSD, and ERC-20 BTC; finalize an expired bound series; add or withdraw native collateral; borrow or repay; and close the trove. There is no function that spends reserved series collateral.

Deployment limits

What is not demonstrated.

The vault is live, but the full writer lifecycle is not complete. These gaps are part of the submission record, not footnotes to hide.

A
Trove closure is blocked by balances.

The latest documented shortfall exceeds 1,500 MUSD. No closure transaction is claimed.

B
Main-series expiry is in the future.

The short-dated demo expired and released MUSD, but the main series cannot finalize before 24 Nov 2026.

C
No audit or independent review.

The contract suite is strong evidence of intended behavior, not a security guarantee.
