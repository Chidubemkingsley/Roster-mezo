01 · The instrument

A put reduced to fixed terms.

Roster does not run a funding-rate perpetual or an oracle-settled option. Each series fixes its strike, premium, expiry, lot size, and settlement path before it accepts a single MUSD.

See the live series
FIXED 01

Strike is immutable.

The series stores K_raw at deployment. Every lot settles against the same 80,000 MUSD/BTC strike; no governance vote can move it.

FIXED 02

Expiry is a timestamp.

Exercise is manual and only available before the expiry timestamp. There is no auto-exercise and no settlement oracle deciding the payoff after the fact.

FIXED 03

The exit is an ask book.

A holder can escrow lots into a public ask. Buyers pay MUSD directly to the seller. There is no bid side, matching engine, or liquidity promise.

02 · Mechanism

Four contracts. One payoff.

The factory, series, order book, and trove vault are independent. The vault is bound to one series, but the series does not depend on the vault to settle an exercise. That separation is deliberate.

01

Register

The factory creates an immutable series with a writer, MUSD, ERC-20 BTC, strike, premium, expiry, name, and symbol.

02

Fund

A writer deposits MUSD. The series reserves the full strike payout for every lot sold and keeps the unused balance as excess.

03

Buy

A buyer pays the fixed MUSD premium and receives position tokens. Each whole lot represents 0.000001 BTC of notional.

04

Exercise or exit

Before expiry, a holder can deliver ERC-20 BTC for the strike in MUSD, or list the position in the secondary ask book.

04 · Asset separation

Native BTC is not ERC-20 BTC.

Roster touches two different BTC representations. They have different roles, different custody paths, and no automatic conversion inside this application.

TROVE COLLATERAL

Native BTC

Held by Mezo against the writer's MUSD debt. It secures the trove and is exposed to liquidation if the collateral ratio falls. Roster never treats it as exercise inventory.

Role: debt backing · Not delivered on exercise
EXERCISE ASSET

ERC-20 BTC

The token at 0x7b7C…0000 that a holder approves and delivers to the writer on exercise. The holder must already own it. The series checks the balance and allowance before paying the strike.

Role: physical delivery · Must be acquired separately
No conversion path is promised.

The application does not wrap, swap, bridge, or mint BTC. A native-BTC trove position does not create ERC-20 BTC for exercise. Any conversion would be an external action with its own counterparty and risk.

05 · Writer vault

The trove is the writer. The debt is real.

RosterTroveWriter opens a Mezo trove with native BTC, borrows MUSD, and deposits part of the borrowed MUSD into its bound series. The vault tracks idle MUSD, series reserve, and ERC-20 BTC separately.

Open the vault page
01
Collateral is locked.

Native BTC in the trove is not spendable by the series or by position holders.

02
Debt accrues interest.

The snapshot debt moves over time. Closing the trove requires repaying the full outstanding MUSD debt.

03
Liquidation is possible.

If BTC falls or debt rises enough, the trove can be liquidated. The application displays the live ratio but cannot prevent it.

04
The reserve has one job.

MUSD deposited into the series backs strike payouts. It is not a yield product, an insurance fund, or a promise of free collateral.

WHAT THE VAULT DOES NOT DO

No auto-repayment. No liquidation shield.

Premiums and expiry releases can be forwarded to the owner, but they do not close trove debt unless the owner explicitly repays. The main series expiry is in the future, so finalization and trove closure remain unproven on this deployment.

06 · Secondary market

An ask book, not a promise of liquidity.

Sellers escrow position lots at a MUSD price. Buyers fill all remaining lots in one transaction. The book never custodies MUSD and never matches against a bid.

Open the order book
ASK ONLY

No synthetic bid.

There is no bid side and no market maker. The best available exit is an actual seller's ask.

ESCROWED

Lots leave the wallet.

Listing transfers position tokens into the order book. Escrowed lots cannot be exercised until cancelled.

DIRECT

MUSD goes to the seller.

The buyer approves MUSD to the order book; the fill pays the seller directly. Roster takes no fee.

FROZEN

Maturity closes the book.

Listings must be cancelled before maturity. Position transfers freeze at expiry, listed or not.

07 · Lifecycle

From registration to final release.

The live deployment has completed creation, funding, purchase, exercise, listing, fill, and one short-dated expiry demonstration. Main-series expiry, finalization, and trove closure have not happened.

STEP 01 · DONE Series created

Factory registers immutable terms and a writer.

STEP 02 · DONE Reserve funded

Writer deposits MUSD; every sold lot reserves its full strike.

STEP 03 · DONE Positions purchased

Buyer pays premium and receives position tokens.

STEP 04 · DONE Exercise and secondary fill

ERC-20 BTC delivered; MUSD strike paid; an ask filled for MUSD.

STEP 05 · PENDING Expiry and finalization

After 24 Nov 2026, expiry releases remaining MUSD to the writer.

STEP 06 · NOT DONE Trove closure

Requires repaying full MUSD debt; currently short of the required balance.

What has actually been demonstrated.

A live deployment, real purchases, an exercise, a secondary fill, a short-dated expiry, and an 82-test contract suite. No browser wallet transaction, audit, organic demand, main-series expiry, or trove closure is claimed. The generated demo buyer is not evidence of market demand.

01
Testnet prototype, not audited.

No audit or independent security review has been completed. Do not use with real funds.

02
Two different BTC assets.

Native BTC trove collateral and the ERC-20 BTC used for exercise are separate. Roster does not convert between them.

03
Trove debt is an obligation.

Interest accrues and liquidation is possible. Series collateral does not repay the trove automatically.
