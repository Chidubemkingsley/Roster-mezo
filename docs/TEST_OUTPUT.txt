The borrowing-fee tests model Mezo's observed 0.1% fee: on a 2,000 MUSD borrow,
debt is 2,202 MUSD and close repayment is 2,002 MUSD. The vault must gross-fund
1,502 MUSD because 1,500 MUSD is reserved for the expired series; finalization
returns that 1,500 MUSD, so the owner's net cost is the 2 MUSD fee.

8. Invariants
===============================================================================

SeriesInvariantTest covers six properties:

  invariant_collateral_split
  invariant_reserve_matches_active_lots
  invariant_supply_equals_active_lots_until_finalization
  invariant_finalized_counters_and_supply
  invariant_audit_identity
  invariant_musd_solvency_at_action_boundaries

The handler fuzzes deposit, purchase, exercise, transfer, approval, transferFrom,
premium withdrawal, maturity, and finalization over a fixed actor set. Expected
reverts are caught as low-level call failures; invariants are checked at successful
action boundaries.
